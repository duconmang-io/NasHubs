Questions

Is NasHubs really free?

Yes. NasHubs sells no plans and asks for no payment details. Pro features unlock for free: sign in with Google in the app, tap Request Pro, and they open once the request is approved.

Is NasHubs an official Synology app?

No. NasHubs is an independent app that talks to your NAS through DSM's own web API. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Synology Inc.

Does my data go through a NasHubs server?

No. Your phone connects straight to your NAS on your network, your own domain, or through NasHubs Agent over Cloudflare Tunnel or Tailscale. Files, photos and admin commands never pass through a NasHubs server.

Which Synology models and DSM versions work?

NasHubs is built for DSM 7 and tested on Synology DS918+. Most models that run DSM 7 work; Container Manager features need a model that supports it.

Can I manage Docker containers from my phone?

Yes. NasHubs lists your containers and packages, starts and stops them, and installs popular self-hosted apps such as Jellyfin, Immich and Pi-hole.

How do I access my NAS remotely without opening ports?

Install NasHubs Agent on the NAS. It signs every command with Ed25519 over pinned TLS and reaches you through Cloudflare Tunnel or Tailscale, so no router ports need to be opened.
